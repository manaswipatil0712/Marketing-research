# Marketing-research

## Project description
This project investigates the key factors that drive smartphone purchase decisions, focusing on elements such as brand preference, pricing, screen dimensions, and battery performance. By analyzing these aspects, the study aims to uncover the priorities and trade-offs consumers make when selecting a smartphone, providing valuable insights into market trends and consumer behavior.

## Data Used

The data was generated through a simulated survey of 264 participants.

## Individual contribution

Conducted a detailed factor and cluster analysis to identify key drivers of smartphone purchasing behavior and segment customers based on their preferences. Through factor analysis, identified two primary components: Performance and Utility and Aesthetic and Design, as well as Social Status & Identity and Price Sensitivity & Tech Enthusiasm, which revealed distinct customer priorities. Cluster analysis further segmented consumers into Style-Conscious Buyers, Balanced Buyers, Performance Enthusiasts, Social Expressives, Value Seekers, Practical Minimalists, and Tech Enthusiasts. This work provided actionable insights into how different customer groups prioritize technical performance, aesthetics, social identity, and price in their decision-making processes, helping to inform targeted product and marketing strategies.

## Outcomes/ Recommendations

Our recommendations focus on tailoring smartphone features and marketing strategies to align with the preferences of distinct customer segments:  

1. **Style-Conscious Buyers**: Emphasize sleek designs, trendy features, and brand value in marketing campaigns. Highlight aesthetics and portability.  
2. **Balanced Buyers**: Offer products that strike a balance between functionality and design, appealing to customers seeking both utility and style.  
3. **Performance Enthusiasts**: Focus on promoting technical specifications, durability, and robust performance. Target customers who prioritize reliability over appearance.  
4. **Social Expressives**: Market products as symbols of status and identity, leveraging brand prestige and fashion-forward designs.  
5. **Value Seekers**: Highlight affordability, discounts, and value-for-money features to attract cost-conscious customers.  
6. **Practical Minimalists**: Provide simple, functional devices without unnecessary features, targeting efficiency-focused users.  
7. **Tech Enthusiasts**: Showcase innovative features and cutting-edge technology to capture the attention of innovation-driven customers.
